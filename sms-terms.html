What messages we send

If you consent to receive conversational and informational SMS from ROAM Investments LLC, you agree to receive conversational and informational SMS from us. These messages include:

  • Conversational. You text one of our parks with a question and a member of our team replies.
  • Informational. Reservation confirmations and changes, arrival instructions and gate codes, site assignments, billing and balance notices, and notices about park conditions or closures.

We do not send promotional or marketing text messages under this program.

How you consent

You consent to receive text messages from us by giving us your mobile number and agreeing to be contacted by text. You may do this at the time you book, in person at a park, over the phone, or on a written or online form.

Consent is optional. You are not required to agree to receive text messages in order to make a reservation or stay with us. We keep a record of your consent and of any request to stop.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message from us and we will stop sending text messages to that number. You will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed. You can also ask us to stop by calling or emailing us.

If you opt out, we may still contact you by phone, email or post about a reservation you have made.
